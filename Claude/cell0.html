Showing
Pillars, not cells. Six seeds run headless gave 5.2 enclosed regions against 0.8 for a control world with the same power spectrum and the same values, so the geometry is real. But what grows is filled pillars with dark halos, which is where ant nests start too. Walls between pillars are the next rung. Drag V0 to zero to cut the material's grip on the waves: coverage jumps from 18% to 61% because nothing chokes the growth any more. That is the check that the coupling is load-bearing at all.
