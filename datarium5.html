Stopping line

The body is pinned in this gate so internal cause and effect cannot be confused with locomotion. Fibres do not yet travel with a moving body or survive fission. If the exact fibre arrangement does not change held-out I/O against isotropic, shuffled and erased attackers, there is no organ yet.
